# TipidHome
### Family Utility & Expense Log

## About the Project

**TipidHome** is a mobile application designed to help families manage household expenses, monitor utility bills, track budgets, and develop better saving habits.

The app provides a simple and organized way to monitor household finances, manage bill payments, and reduce unnecessary spending.

## Objectives

- Help families track daily expenses and household spending.
- Monitor monthly budgets and utility bills.
- Provide reminders for upcoming bill payments.
- Generate reports to understand spending patterns.
- Encourage savings and environmentally friendly habits.
- Make household financial management easier and more organized.

## Features

### 1. Landing Page
- User login and account registration.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.

### 2. Home
- Overview of the monthly household budget.
- Summary of total expenses and utility costs.
- Display of savings and upcoming bills.
- Recent transaction activity.

### 3. Expenses
- Record and manage household expenses.
- Organize transactions by spending category.
- Track expenses such as groceries, transportation, shopping, and health.
- View transaction history.

### 4. Utilities
- Monitor water, electricity, and internet bills.
- Record utility expenses and due dates.
- Set reminders for upcoming payments.
- Track bill payment status.

### 5. Reports
- View monthly spending charts.
- Compare actual expenses with the planned budget.
- Analyze spending by category.
- Monitor household spending trends.

### 6. Save Green
- Discover practical money-saving tips.
- Develop water and electricity conservation habits.
- Reduce household waste.
- Track simple eco-friendly daily habits.

### 7. Account
- Manage personal account details.
- Manage household members.
- Configure budget settings and notifications.
- Access help and support.
- Log out securely.

## Target Users

TipidHome is designed for:
- Families managing shared household expenses.
- Individuals responsible for paying utility bills.
- Households that want to improve their budgeting habits.
- Users who want to save money and reduce waste.

## Benefits

- **Better Budgeting:** Helps families plan and monitor their expenses.
- **Organized Bills:** Keeps utility information and due dates in one place.
- **Spending Awareness:** Helps users understand where their money goes.
- **Improved Savings:** Encourages practical ways to reduce expenses.
- **Sustainable Living:** Promotes responsible use of household resources.
